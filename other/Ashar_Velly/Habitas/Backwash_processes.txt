Backwash (2min)
1. Open valve
2. Open drain line
3. Close motor and Geyser
4. Switch valve to backwash
5. Start one motor

Rins (1min)
6. Stop motor
7. Switch value to Rins
8. Start one motor again
9. After everything finish, switch valve from Rins to Normal close drain valve, close output value, switch on Geyser, switch all motor to auto.
